    About Us

    Guangzhou Yuexin Lighting Co., Ltd. is a China-based manufacturer dedicated to professional stage lighting. We design, produce and export moving head lights, LED wash / effect lights, laser lights, stage effect machines and lighting controllers.

    Your one-stop stage lighting factory — everything you need, under one roof.

    We know lighting buyers don't want to juggle five different suppliers. At Yuexin, we cover the full range: moving heads, LED pars and wash lights, effect lights, lasers, hazers / foggers / bubbles and DMX consoles — all designed and built in our own factory.

    • Full product range — moving head, LED wash, LED effect, laser, stage effects and controllers.
    • One-stop sourcing — mix and match categories in one order, one shipment, one contact.
    • OEM / ODM — custom housing, logo, label, packaging and firmware to your brand.
    • CE / RoHS certified — strict QC, every fixture tested before packing.

    Whether you are a distributor, rental company, installation contractor or event production house, our sales and engineering team speaks your language. We help you spec the right rig, quote competitive factory-direct pricing, and deliver on time. Long-term partnership over one-off sales — that's how we work.

    Why Choose Yuexin

    We built the factory the way a lighting buyer wants it: one order, one factory, one accountable team — no middlemen, no finger-pointing.

    One-Stop Procurement

    Buy moving heads, LEDs, lasers, effects and controllers in a single order. We consolidate, pack and ship them together — your container, your project, simplified.

    OEM / ODM Customization

    Customize housing color, lens, DMX channels, logo, retail box and manual. We also develop new fixtures to your design for exclusive distribution.

    Strict Quality Control

    100% aging test, photometer and DMX address check on every unit. CE, RoHS certified; we support SGS / TUV third-party inspection before shipment.

    Fast Lead Time

    Sample within 5–7 days, bulk order in 20–30 days. Rush production available for event deadlines and expo orders.

    Factory-Direct Pricing

    No trading-company markup. Ex-works pricing with tiered volume discounts, so your margin stays competitive in your local market.

    After-Sales Support

    2-year warranty standard. English-speaking sales team, video troubleshooting, spare parts policy — we stand behind every fixture we ship.

YX-MH1915B — 19×15W Large Bee Eye Moving Head

Small size, light weight, high brightness with thick heat-dissipating aluminum housing and long-lifespan LEDs — at a highly competitive price. 1:1 Clay Paky compatible channel modes.

  • Input Voltage: AC100-240V, 50/60Hz
  • Rated Power: 300W @ 220V
  • Light Source: 19 × 15W RGBW 4-in-1 high-brightness LED beads
  • Color: RGBW
  • Dimming: 0-100% linear adjustment
  • Strobe: Electronic 0.3-20/s; pulse strobe
  • Control: Single fixture independent control
  • Pan / Tilt: 540° / 180° with auto-return
  • DMX Channels: 21/23/35/78/92/97/99CH (1:1 Clay Paky)
  • Zoom: 5°-55°
  • Effects: Wash, beam & effect modes (vortex, kaleidoscope); front mirror disc rotates 360°
  • Housing: High-temp resistant engineering plastic, IP20
